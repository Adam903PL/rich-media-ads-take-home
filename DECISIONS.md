# Decision log

Short entries, newest at the bottom. Bigger decisions link to a detailed record in [`docs/decisions/`](docs/decisions/).

Format: **D-XXX Topic:** decision — why ([details](docs/decisions/D-XXX-topic.md))

## Setup

- **D-001 Tooling:** no bundler, plain HTML/CSS/ES modules served by `npx serve` — the brief allows it and it keeps the shipped ad free of build artefacts.
- **D-002 Dependencies on demand:** GSAP and Playwright are added in the first step that needs them, not up front.
- **D-003 Workflow:** reduced Maister setup — project docs, a trimmed `global` set plus custom `ads` standards, and `quick-plan`/`quick-dev` per roadmap step instead of the full development orchestrator. This keeps the process overhead proportional to a 4-day task while the brief's rules are still checked at every step.
- **D-004 Weight budget:** the fireplace counts as one ad, so `top`, `left` and `right` together stay under 3 MB. This is the stricter reading of "total ad (all files you ship)", and it is achievable.

## Variant A

- **D-005 Viewport scaling:** contain-fit the 360×640 stage to the visible `100dvh` area and fill the spare strips with the stage's measured edge colours. The whole design is always visible and gameplay is identical on every phone ([details](docs/decisions/D-005-viewport-scaling.md)).
- **D-006 Renderer:** the moving scene (gut, obstacles, player) is DOM `<img>` elements moved with `transform`, not canvas or Phaser. It shares one scaling model with the static layers and stays crisp at every pixel ratio, and collision uses alpha masks that do not depend on the renderer ([details](docs/decisions/D-006-moving-scene-renderer.md)).
- **D-007 Start screen without obstacles:** the brief lists the start screen as logo bar, headline and tutorial, so the obstacles shown in the mockup are treated as illustration. They first appear during the run.
- **D-008 Playfield bounds:** the clouds are walls. Since they are static and the player's x is fixed, the exact alpha-mask collision reduces to two limits for the player's top edge, 232.5 and 533.5, computed by `tools/audit/cloud-limits.mjs` ([details](docs/decisions/D-008-playfield-bounds.md)).
- **D-009 Physics and start:** ~15 s run (145 px/s), gravity 1600 px/s², jump −420 px/s (≈ 55 px). The first tap glides the player from its design position, only 26 px below the ceiling, to the playfield centre in 0.6 s before control starts. Space and ArrowUp work like a tap ([details](docs/decisions/D-009-physics-and-start.md)).
- **D-010 Obstacle layout:** a fixed, designed layout of the 15 obstacles on the gut's tubes. `tools/audit/level-check.mjs` checks it against tube, band and room rules, propagates reachable heights at human tapping speed and marks dead ends. A planning bot won every run ([details](docs/decisions/D-010-obstacle-layout.md)).
- **D-011 Collision:** alpha masks from the images on the 2x grid; a box test first, then pixels. The player's mask is eroded by 2 px for a slightly forgiving hit, and the same rule recomputes the cloud limits (now 230 / 535.5). Rectangles were rejected because empty corners would kill ([details](docs/decisions/D-011-collision.md)).
- **D-012 Loading GSAP:** `vendor/gsap.min.js` is loaded by a classic `defer` script before the module scripts, and the modules use the global `gsap`. It is one 72.9 KB file, byte-identical to the npm release. The package's ES module entry would mean three unminified files (237.5 KB) without a bundler.
- **D-013 Endcards and replay:** each endcard is a full-screen layer above the stage and its caps, with its own logo bar and a navy bottom cap. After a 0.6 s (fail) / 0.4 s (win) hold on the crash or finish, a ~1.9 s GSAP entrance plays with the buttons last. Replay returns to the start screen: `destroy()` removes the run's loop and listeners, and the entrance timelines are rewound. The endcard covers the frozen game on every phone, the player chooses when the next run starts, and a replay leaves nothing running.
- **D-014 Audio:** Web Audio with buffers decoded during the preload, an `AudioContext` created on the first gesture that grants activation (`pointerup`, not `pointerdown`), a music loop found in the decoded buffer (36 bars of 57.6 s, seam at a zero crossing), per-sound gains from measured loudness, and an inline SVG mute toggle. This removes the file's 208 ms loop gap in every decoder, unlocks on phones, and levels sounds that differed by 11 LU ([details](docs/decisions/D-014-audio.md)).
- **D-015 Party cone on the win endcard:** a second copy of `konfetti.png` above the happy poo, clipped with CSS to the party cone (x 157–169.5, y 333–353), and animated together with the confetti layer. In the mockup the cone sits in front of the poo while the rest of the confetti stays behind it, which no single layer order can show. The asset file stays unchanged, and the win graphics go from 40.3 to 50.5 dB against the mockup.
- **D-016 Happy poo inside the bubble (deviation from the mockup):** the win endcard's poo, and its party cone with it, sits 31.5 px higher than in `03-win-endcard.png`. In the mockup 8.8% of the poo hangs over the bubble's lower edge, which read as misplaced in review; 31.5 px is the smallest lift that keeps the whole poo inside the bubble with an 8 px margin, measured on the alpha masks. It knowingly departs from the brief's rule to match the design positions, and a short piece of the streamer now shows below the poo.
- **D-017 Start screen intro, no speed ramp:** a 1.25 s GSAP intro (the gut fades in under the clouds, the headline rises, the poo pops in, the hand arrives), followed by a tapping-hand loop that ends before 30 s. A wrapper around the player takes the pop, so the intro and the game never write the same element's transform. The first tap jumps the intro to its end, and replay plays it again. The stage is revealed only after the game listens. The speed ramp is cut: the obstacle layout already ramps (mean gap 130 px in the first half, 115 px in the second, both gates in the second half, three tall words to finish), and a speed change would invalidate the layout proof of D-010.
- **D-018 Sound effects re-encoded:** the four 320 kbps effects are re-encoded with LAME, `ffmpeg -i <file> -map_metadata -1 -c:a libmp3lame -b:a 96k -ac 1` for the crash and the cheer (side/mid −20 and −31 dB, practically mono) and `-b:a 128k -ac 2` for fail and applause (real stereo). Audio goes from 1 369 577 to 814 881 B, and the ad from 2 129 475 to 1 574 779 B. The gains in `audio.js` are corrected by the measured loudness change (≤ 0.4 LU), so the −14 / −18 LUFS targets still hold. The music stays as delivered: it is already 64 kbps, it is real stereo, and a lower rate would be heard in a loop that plays through every run.
- **D-019 PNGs recompressed losslessly:** each PNG is re-encoded with `ffmpeg -c:v png -pred {none,paeth,mixed} -compression_level 9 -pix_fmt rgba`. The smallest result replaces the file only if it is smaller and its decoded RGBA pixels hash the same as the original (`framemd5`). The `pHYs` chunk that `ffmpeg` adds (a malformed 0:1 aspect ratio) is stripped, so the files keep the originals' IHDR/IDAT/IEND structure. 36 of 37 files shrink (`gameplay.png` does not): 640 055 → 574 516 B. The ad goes from 1 574 779 to 1 509 240 B (1.44 MB, 48% of the 3 MB budget). `ffmpeg` was already in the toolchain and no dedicated optimiser was installed, so no new dependency was added.

## Variant B

- **D-020 Background source:** each part gets its own crop of `bg-big.jpg` (left x 0–368, top x 320–1360 × y 0–272, right x 1312–1680, in fireplace CSS px), re-encoded with `ffmpeg -c:v mjpeg -q:v 2` at 48.4–49.7 dB; `bg-big.jpg` and the per-part `bg.jpg` files leave the shipped folder. `bg-big.jpg` is the only source whose seams line up, and the extra moss on the side facing the fireplace centre lets all three backgrounds zoom around one point (840, 487.5) up to 1.10 without breaking the seams. The backgrounds go from 3 406 018 to 1 235 592 B; the packshots differ from the layout and that is still open with the client (Q3) ([details](docs/decisions/D-020-background-source.md)).
- **D-021 Transparency restored:** `box.png`, `cta.png`, `left/hl.png` and `right/hl.png` get a one-entry `tRNS` chunk right after `PLTE` that makes palette index 0 (black, used only for the background) fully transparent. All four lost that chunk in export, so browsers showed them on solid black; the pixel data is untouched (decoded RGB identical, alpha 0 exactly on the formerly black pixels, e.g. the CTA's 168 corner pixels). The anti-aliasing levels (indices 1–9 of the copy, 1–6 of the CTA corners) cannot be recovered from these files, so the copy keeps slightly harder edges than in the layout (Q2). Each file grows by 13 B: 326 → 339, 1 421 → 1 434, 5 335 → 5 348, 4 786 → 4 799.
- **D-022 Part documents:** `top.html`, `left.html` and `right.html` are standalone documents rendered 1:1 at their slot size, and they share one stylesheet and one module keyed on `data-part`. The ad server's iframe has exactly the slot's size, so nothing needs scaling, and one copy of the shared code keeps the three parts consistent. Delivered as three separate ads, each package carries its own copy of the shared files (and of GSAP from B4); B7 reports the weight both ways.
- **D-023 Text panel and CTA corners (default of Q2):** the panel (`box.png`) is shown at opacity 0.9 as in the layout, in the asset's colour, and the CTA is clipped with a 7 px `border-radius`. The radius comes from the corner pixels `cta.png` lost with its `tRNS` chunk: in all four corners they trace a circle of 13.75 px @2x. The clip runs through the former anti-aliasing pixels, so the corners render smooth instead of stepped.
- **D-024 Preview page:** `preview.html` places the three parts in iframes at their fireplace positions (x 0 / 324 / 1360, 4 px gaps) on the layout's `#282828`, with the website area left empty, and scales the whole 1680×975 fireplace to the window, never above 1:1, with whole-pixel offsets. A reviewer on a 1366–1536 px laptop sees the entire fireplace at once, and at full size the parts render exactly as on their own (68–83 dB against the standalone pages). `?overlay=1` lays one `layout.png` over the whole fireplace through the parts' own `showDesignOverlay`. The preview is a review tool and not part of the shipped ad, and its iframes are not sandboxed, because module scripts from an opaque origin would need CORS headers the dev server does not send.
- **D-025 Intro:** a 3.5 s GSAP timeline per part, on beats shared by all three parts. The moss fades in and settles from scale 1.08 to 1 around the fireplace centre (0–3.5 s). The top headline builds line by line (0.35 / 0.70 / 1.05 s), the logos pop in (0.45 s), the panels open upwards (1.2 s), the copy rises (1.6 s) and the CTAs come last (2.2–2.7 s).
  - **The background settles rather than zooms in.** The end state has to be the layout, and a scale below 1 has no moss to show (D-020); a zoom in to 1.05 would end with the packshots ~35 px further out.
  - **The headline lines are three copies of `top/hl.png` with static gradient masks** that fade across the gaps between the lines (y 78–92 and 145.5–159.5). The soft shadow runs through those gaps at 20–30% opacity, so a hard clip would have drawn a straight edge in it. Stacked copies are up to ~4% lighter where the masks blend, so when the last line lands, the copies give way to the single full image and the end state is the asset itself.
  - The product cannot slide in: it is part of the background (P4), so it settles with the moss.
  - Only `transform` and `opacity` are animated. With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the timeline jumps to its end, and the animations pause while the page is hidden.
- **D-026 Shared start:** a part in a frame posts `fireplace:ready` to its parent after its preload. The preview answers `fireplace:hold` at once, and when all three are ready sends each the same `fireplace:start` time, 150 ms ahead, on a clock all documents share (`performance.timeOrigin + performance.now()`). Each part waits for that moment with `gsap.delayedCall`, so a hidden page also holds the start; a part that gets the time late starts that far into the intro. In the preview the three intros run within 0–4 ms of each other (6 loads), so the shared zoom stays one surface.
  - **A part without a partner still starts:** at once as a top-level page, and after 300 ms in a frame that never answers, such as an ad server's. A partner answers within milliseconds, so the 300 ms are only paid in production.
  - A part only accepts messages from its parent, and the preview only from its own frames and origin. Parts post with `targetOrigin '*'`, because an ad server's origin is not known in advance, and the message carries no data.
  - With reduced motion there is nothing to start together, so the parts show their end state at once and send nothing.
  - If a part fails to load, it reports the file and the preview keeps the others waiting, rather than starting an incomplete fireplace.
- **D-027 Idle motion and clickout:** after the intro, the same timeline carries a calm phase, so the parts stay in step.
  - **The moss slowly zooms in to 1.025 and back** over 22 s (`sine.inOut`) around the fireplace centre. That is the brief's "background slowly zooms in", and it still ends at the layout. A sideways drift is not possible, because the outer edges of the crops have no extra moss (D-020).
  - **The CTAs pulse gently five times** (scale 1.05, 1.2 s each, 4.5 s apart).
  - All motion ends 25.5 s after the start, inside the brief's ~30 s. A hidden page holds the timeline, and with reduced motion none of it plays.
  - **Each part is one transparent `<button>` over its whole area.** A click anywhere, or Enter / Space, opens `window.clickTag`, read at click time, or `https://example.com/landing` in a new window. Its German `aria-label` repeats the CTA and the part's copy, and an inset white focus ring with a dark shadow shows on light and dark moss. A button rather than a link, because the URL is only known at click time; Variant A's CTAs work the same way.
- **D-028 Variant B PNGs recompressed losslessly:** the D-019 method (`ffmpeg -c:v png -pred {none,paeth,mixed} -compression_level 9 -pix_fmt rgba`, the smallest result kept only if smaller and its decoded RGBA hashes the same, then the `pHYs` chunk that `ffmpeg` adds stripped). `top/hl.png` 194 672 → 193 707 B (`mixed`), each `logo.png` 12 981 → 11 480 B (`none`); 220 634 → 216 667 B in all. The palette PNGs (`box.png`, `cta.png`, both `hl.png` copies) do not get smaller and stay as repaired in D-021. The gain is small (0.25% of the variant); it is done for consistency with Variant A, not to meet the budget.
- **D-029 Variant B weight:** each part ships as its own package: its HTML, `css/style.css`, the statically imported modules, `vendor/gsap.min.js` and its own assets. Top 713 440 B, left 517 091 B, right 495 791 B: **1 726 322 B (1.65 MB) for all three packages**, 55% of the 3 MB budget, with the shared files counted once per part. Counted once, the same files weigh 1 553 059 B (1.48 MB). The backgrounds are 80% of it (D-020); GSAP is 73 KB per package. The review tools are not shipped: `design/`, `preview.html` with its CSS and JS, and `js/debug.js` (4 164 B). `tools/audit/weight.mjs` reproduces the numbers from the pages' own references.
