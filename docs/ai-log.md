# AI log

How AI tools were used, and every case where their output was wrong and had to be corrected. The README's AI note is picked from this list at the end.

**Tools:**
- Claude Code (Opus) for asset analysis, planning, code and docs.
- The Maister plugin, in a reduced setup: project docs and standards, `quick-plan` / `quick-dev`.

| # | Date | Stage | What the AI did | What was wrong | How it was caught / fixed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2026-11-04 | Asset audit | Took the brief's statement that the per-part `bg.jpg` files are "pre-cut versions" of `bg-big.jpg` at face value and planned to crop at fixed offsets. | They are different composites: different packshot placement, different crop of the top and different brightness. The first automated match also returned meaningless offsets. | Side-by-side crops and comparisons against `layout.png` → documented as P3 in the asset audit; the background choice became an explicit decision and question Q3. |
| 2 | 2026-11-04 | Asset audit | Composited the left skyscraper with the CTA at a guessed position (y 1592 @2x). | The CTA overlapped the copy. | Template-matched `cta.png` against the layout rescaled to 2x → measured y 1677 @2x (838.5 CSS). Positions are now only taken from measurements. |
| 3 | 2026-11-04 | Recruiter questions | Concluded from whole-image matching that all per-part backgrounds are "different composites" from `bg-big.jpg`. | Overstated: the left part is the same image cropped 2 × 6 px off. Whole-image matching could not find it because both files are 1950 px tall, which left no room for a vertical offset. | Re-measured with small moss and packshot crops at several scales → exact relation per part in asset-audit P3. The email states measured numbers instead of the general claim. |
| 4 | 2026-11-04 | Recruiter questions | Checked for CRLF line endings with `grep -rl $'\r'` and reported "no CRLF". | The check was unreliable in this shell in both directions: it missed real CRs in a working copy, then flagged clean files. | Replaced with a byte count (`tr -cd '\r' < file \| wc -c`); all committed files are LF. |
| 5 | 2026-11-04 | A1 | Planned to fill the spare strips around the scaled stage with a background gradient on the root. | At fractional scales each layer's edge is anti-aliased on its own, so the navy `bg.jpg` showed through as a hairline at the top and bottom edges (visible at 390×844). | Caught in the verification screenshot. Replaced the gradient with stage caps that overlap the stage by 1 px; pixel rows at the seams are now uniform. |
| 6 | 2026-11-05 | A2 | Placed the full-frame gut at 0,0 because the audit said its edge "matches the design at offset 0" — a visual judgement, never measured. While measuring it, read vertical offsets from small crops and composited candidates in YUV. | The design's gut sits at x −2. Small crops of flat tube areas and vertical walls matched at many positions (one crop "found" +9 px vertically). ffmpeg `overlay` on a YUV 4:2:0 base rounds positions to even pixels, so odd candidates scored identically. | The pixel diff showed the tube outlines doubled. Ranking whole-frame composites in RGB settled it: (−4, 0) @2x = 24.9 dB, all neighbours lower. The audit was corrected. |
| 7 | 2026-11-05 | A2 | Clipped the scene exactly at the headline band edge (168.5 px), as planned. | That row of `border.png` is anti-aliased, so the middle tube showed through as a line under the band. | Measured the fully opaque rows of `border.png` (169–204.5) and moved the clip to 172 px, inside them. The "headline + clouds" band went from 35.7 to 41.5 dB. |
| 8 | 2026-11-05 | A3 planning | Proposed cloud walls as straight lines at the edges of the opaque cloud strips (y 204.5 / 598.5) and a ~63 px jump, from whole-width measurements. | At the player's columns the top clouds hang down to y 234.5. The design start is only 26 px below that, so the first jump would have hit the ceiling. The lines would also have let the player sink visibly into the clouds. | Measured the cloud edges at the player's columns and computed exact limits by testing the player mask against the cloud mask (232.5 / 533.5). This led to the start glide (D-009). |
| 9 | 2026-11-05 | A3 verification | Tested held-key repeats right after a real jump and reported "repeats not ignored". | The test was wrong, not the code: the player was still rising from the real jump, so the repeats could not be told apart. | Re-ran it with the repeats sent while the player was falling: the fall continued (repeats ignored) and a real press still jumped. |
| 10 | 2026-11-05 | A4 planning | Hand-drew an obstacle layout from coarse 1x tube measurements, with a 30 px minimum corridor. | Several positions were off the tubes. One stretch was impassable: a ~100 px climb in 0.27 s. A 30 px corridor ignores the 55 px bob of a tapping player. | Wrote `level-check.mjs`: 2x tube mask, a corridor of one jump arc plus 10 px, and reachability at human climb speed. Iterated until it passed, then added a backward pass that exposes dead ends (bac4 "above"). |
| 11 | 2026-11-05 | A4 verification | Wrote browser bots to prove the run can be won. They failed at Blut, bac3, Tabu and Couch-Potato. | The bots were wrong, not the layout: too short a lookahead, a corridor rule laxer than the checker's, then a greedy choice, then intersecting corridors that never apply at the same time. | Replaced them with a planning bot that runs the checker's backward/forward logic and treats near-simultaneous obstacles as gates. It won 2/2 runs with 31 human-rate taps. |
| 12 | 2026-11-05 | A4 verification | Reported "4.6 µs per collision check, worst case". | The test case actually hit on every call, so it exited early: the cheapest case, not the worst. | Searched for placements where the boxes overlap the most but the shapes do not touch, and measured 5.5–9 µs per check. |
| 13 | 2026-11-06 | A5 planning | Expected the finished endcards to match the mockups almost pixel for pixel, citing the audit's "rebuild check". | The rebuild check only covered the graphic layers. The headline and button PNGs are typeset differently from the mockups, so the whole frame scores 18.6 dB however exact the build is. | Compared the build with a composite of the delivered layers (40.2 dB) and that composite with the mockup, band by band. This separated build errors from asset differences. Audit §2.3 now states which layers match. |
| 14 | 2026-11-06 | A5 verification | Tested replay with `?debug=1` by tapping as soon as the stage became visible, and got no start. | The test was wrong, not the replay: in debug mode the stage is revealed before `debug.js` has loaded, and the game only listens after that. | Retried 300 ms after the reveal: the debug replay passes. The reveal gap is a separate, debug-only issue. Fixed in A7: the stage is revealed after `startRun()`. |
| 15 | 2026-11-06 | A5–A6 verification | Proved the win path by routing a `level.js` with the obstacles moved off the playfield, then ran further tests in the same browser. | The browser had cached the modified `level.js`. The A5 tests after the win test (replay, listeners, keyboard, reduced motion, hidden tab, viewports, debug) ran without reachable obstacles. Their results still hold, because every one of them ended by falling onto the floor without taps. | Noticed in A6 when the obstacles reported `top: 2000px`. The cache was cleared, the replay checks were rerun with the real layout, and the routed file is now served with `cache-control: no-store`. |
| 16 | 2026-11-06 | A5 | Stacked the win endcard in the audit's layer order (bubble, confetti, poo). In verification it recorded the win graphics at 40.3 dB, against 53.5 dB for fail, without looking into the gap. | In the mockup the party cone sits on the poo's head, in front of it. The build hid it behind the poo, so the user saw the poo as wrong. | The user reported it. A diff against the mockup isolated the cone; a full reorder would have pulled the streamer in front too (36.9 dB). Fixed with a clipped copy of the layer (D-015): 53.4 dB in the browser. |
| 17 | 2026-11-06 | A7 verification | Took intro frames with `page.evaluate((time) => intro.pause(time))` and reported the test as hanging, suspecting the screenshot. | The arrow function returned the GSAP timeline. Playwright then tried to serialise its whole object graph (parent timeline, children, DOM targets) and never finished. The screenshot was fine. | Found by probing the page (intro paused at 0, rAF running, a separate screenshot working). | 18 | 2026-11-06 | A8 | Ran a `while read` loop over the PNG list that called `ffmpeg` for each file. | `ffmpeg` reads stdin by default, so it swallowed the rest of the file list. The loop stopped after 8 files on a garbled path (`game/player.png`). | The broken path in the log gave it away. Added `-nostdin` and reran; then checked every PNG against `HEAD` independently, not through the script's own log. |
| 19 | 2026-11-06 | A8 | Declared the recompressed PNGs equivalent because their decoded pixels hashed the same. | Identical pixels did not mean identical files: `ffmpeg` added a `pHYs` chunk with a malformed 0:1 aspect ratio, which the originals did not have. The game sizes obstacles from `naturalWidth`, so a decoder that honours `pHYs` could have resized them. | Listed the chunks of every file after the run, decoded the `pHYs` values and stripped the chunk losslessly. All 37 files have the original chunk structure again, with identical pixels. |
| 20 | 2026-11-06 | B1 | Filled the audit's table of how far each part's zoom reaches into the background (scale 1.10 around the fireplace centre) with values worked out by hand. | Two of the six reach values were wrong: the left part's outer edge (62.3 instead of 76.4) and the right part's (1617.7 instead of 1603.6). | 21 | 2026-11-06 | B2 verification | Opened the parts as `left.html?overlay=1` and waited for the overlay; the test timed out. | `npx serve` redirects `left.html` to `left` and drops the query string, so the page never saw the flag. | 22 | 2026-11-06 | B2 verification | Planned to prove the build by comparing browser screenshots with an `ffmpeg` composite of the same layers, as in A5, and got only ~30 dB, with 17 dB on the CTA. | The method, not the build: the screenshots are colour-managed (+1 to +4 levels even on the PNG logo), and `ffmpeg` decodes and downscales the moss JPEG differently from Chrome, which costs a lot on such fine texture. | A 1 px offset search settled the geometry (DPR 2 for the skyscrapers, DPR 1 for the top banner): the exact position scores 35.2–36.6 dB in luma and every 1 px shift at most 24.5 dB, in all three parts.
